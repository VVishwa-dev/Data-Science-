THE MATH, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Transparent by
design.

01

Start with your own numbers

Your monthly income and living expenses show what is left after the loan EMIs you entered.

02

Compare two totals

For each loan, we compare its remaining scheduled EMIs with its outstanding balance plus estimated closure charges.

03

Use the ranking as a prompt

Loans with the largest positive difference appear first. A negative difference means the entered payoff cost is higher than the remaining EMIs.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT

A clearer estimate
is still an estimate.

This planner uses simple arithmetic. It does not model amortization schedules, daily interest accrual, tax treatment, foreclosure rules, opportunity cost, or lender-specific settlement terms. Its ranking is not a trained ML prediction, personal financial advice, or an instruction to close a loan.

Before taking action, ask each lender for a current settlement quote, confirm any fees and conditions, and make sure you keep a suitable emergency reserve.
